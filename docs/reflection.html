Tired Integration: The Downfall

In our 39th hour, 7am Sunday, we attempted to complete the process by integrating each component of our architecture in one single step. This oversight caused a shorting of our lead robotic arm. Bringing an end to our Hackathon.

Next time

In our 42 hours our goal was, 1. lose as much sleep and 2. learn as much about Physical AI as possible. We think we did just that, here is some of thoughts on what we would do next time:

Quantify our model improvement: The eye test is exciting, but quantifying training loss against training time would have supported our model choice massively.

Integrate early: The power of decomposition felt intuitively powerful to us, and yet low on time we did not go through with it. Given the opportunity, building confidence on how models interact would have allowed us to break down the process further, allowing us to optimise each model for more complicated tasks.
